About these numbers

Per-user reports
  • Dates on graphs are UTC calendar days.
  • Every itinerary the AI creates is saved to the user's account. The saved count therefore covers generated plans that still exist, not every generation attempt.
  • Itineraries are removed automatically after the trip ends, so older history shrinks over time.
  • Sign-ins and chatbot messages are counted from the day usage tracking was set up. Earlier activity wasn't recorded.
