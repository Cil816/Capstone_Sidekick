Installing on Android

Sidekick is not on the Play Store yet, so Android asks you to allow the install once.

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded Sidekick.apk file.
  3. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser.
  4. Tap Install, then open Sidekick.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sidekick free?

Yes. Sidekick is free to download and use.

Which phones can use it?

Android phones and tablets running Android 7.0 or later. There is no iPhone version yet.

Why does my phone warn me when I install it?

Sidekick is not on the Play Store yet, so Android asks you to allow installing apps from your browser. You only need to allow it once; see Installing on Android.

Does it cover places outside Batangas?

No. Sidekick plans trips only within the province of Batangas, such as Nasugbu, Anilao, Calatagan, Laiya, Taal and Batangas City.

Can I plan a trip for a group?

Yes. Choose from 1 to 10 travelers. Your budget is for the whole group, so Sidekick plans rooms, meals and entrance fees for everyone.

Are the prices and travel times exact?

They are estimates. Prices come from current web results and travel times from Google Maps, and both can change, so check important details before you go.

Can I book hotels in the app?

No. Sidekick shows hotels and restaurants with their prices, ratings, phone numbers and websites, and you book with them directly.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. Planning trips, chatting with the assistant, and loading maps and places all need the internet.

Can I sign in with Google?

Yes. You can create an account with your email address or continue with your Google account.

What happens to my data?

Sidekick collects only what it needs to plan your trips and never sells your data. You can delete your account yourself in Settings. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
