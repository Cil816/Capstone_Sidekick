Cookie & Storage Notice

Effective October 12, 2026 · Sidekick App

In short: Sidekick does not use cookies, advertising, or analytics trackers. It saves some information in your device's app storage so the app works, which is why there is no cookie banner.

1. Cookies and tracking

Sidekick itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking tools. The admin website keeps its sign-in in the browser's session storage, not in a cookie.

2. What Sidekick saves on your device

Sidekick uses your device's local storage and session storage (similar to an app's saved files). These items are needed for the app to work, so they are not optional:

WhatWhyHow long
Sign-in session (name, email, access token)Keep you signed inUntil you sign out (sessions end after 30 days)
Travel preferencesPersonalise suggestionsUntil you reset them or delete your account
Itinerary, chosen hotel, budget estimatesShow your trip, including offlineUntil you remove the itinerary or delete your account
Chat history, including place cardsShow your conversation with SidekickUntil you clear the chat or delete your account
Reminders and notification settingsTrip alertsUntil you change or remove them
Screen state (selected destination, scroll position, unfinished onboarding)Return you to where you wereUsually until you close the app

To remove this data, use Settings to clear the chat, remove the itinerary, or delete your account; sign out and clear the app's data; or uninstall the app.

3. Third-party services that load in the app

  • Google Maps (map views and place photos) and Google Fonts (text fonts) load from Google's servers. Google receives your device's IP address and basic browser information when this happens, under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Links that open Google Maps take you to Google's own site or app, where Google's cookies and policies apply.

If Sidekick ever adds analytics, advertising, or other non-essential cookies or trackers, we will ask for your consent first and update this notice.
