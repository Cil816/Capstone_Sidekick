Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026 · Sidekick App

In short: we collect only what Sidekick needs to plan your trips. We never sell your data. Chat messages and trip details are sent to Google's AI and maps services to answer you. Your password is never stored in readable form. You can ask us to show, correct, or delete your data at any time, and you can delete your account yourself in Settings.

1. Who we are

Sidekick App ("Sidekick", "we", "us") is a travel-planning app for the province of Batangas, Philippines, created by Carl as a student capstone project, based in Santo Tomas, Batangas, Philippines. We are the personal information controller for the data described here.

Privacy contact (Data Protection Officer): Carl — s2023101800@firstasia.edu.ph

This policy follows the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

2. Who can use Sidekick

Sidekick is only for people 18 years old and above. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a minor created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

4. Our legal basis

  • Contract: to provide the account and trip-planning features you sign up for.
  • Consent: given when you register; you may withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the service secure and preventing abuse (for example, rate limits and email checks), in a way that does not override your rights.
  • Legal obligations: when the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

5. Service providers we share data with

We never sell or rent your personal data. We share only what each provider needs to do its job:

  • Google LLC — Gemini API (AI chat and itinerary planning): your chat messages, trip details and preferences. We use the paid service tier, under which Google states it does not use prompts to improve its products.
  • Google LLC — Google Maps Platform (places, photos, routes, maps): place searches, destinations, and starting locations you enter. Maps shown in the app load from Google's servers.
  • Google LLC — Google Fonts: the app loads its fonts from Google's servers, which receive your device's IP address.
  • Reoon (email verification), and ZeroBounce only as a backup: your email address when you register, to check that it can receive email.
  • Our email provider: your email address and the code we send you.

These providers may process data outside the Philippines. We share data with them only for the purposes above and rely on their contractual and security commitments to protect it. We may also disclose information when required by law or a valid order of a court or government authority.

6. Who can see your data at Sidekick

Only the Sidekick administrator can access the admin website. It shows account details (name, email, sign-up and last-active dates, verification status, travel preferences), itineraries, and app ratings, so the service can be monitored and supported. Admin sign-ins are logged.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you delete your account, or we delete it at your request.
  • Itineraries on our server: deleted automatically about 2 days after the trip's end date.
  • Archived copies: when an account or an itinerary is deleted or expires, a copy of its main details (never your password, chats or reminders) is kept in a restricted archive for a limited time, 30 days by default, so administrators can handle questions and support requests. Your own finished trips from this archive are also shown to you under Profile → Past trips. It is then removed for good. So you can plan a finished trip again (“Do this trip again”), the app also keeps a copy of your most recent finished trips on your phone for up to 60 days; removing a trip from Past trips or deleting your account removes that copy too.
  • App ratings: if you delete your account, a star rating and review you submitted stay in Sidekick’s overall app rating, no longer linked to you: they show as from a “Deleted user”, without your name or email.
  • Unfinished registrations: deleted after 24 hours.
  • Verification and reset codes: expire after 10 minutes.
  • Data on your device (chat history, reminders, preferences): until you clear it in Settings, delete your account, sign out and clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

8. How we protect your data

Passwords and codes are stored as one-way hashes, app sessions use random access tokens, the published app connects to the server over HTTPS, and admin access requires separate credentials. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission as required by law (within 72 hours of knowing about it).

9. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is processed (this policy);
  • Access your personal data and get a copy of it;
  • Correct inaccurate data;
  • Object to processing and withdraw consent;
  • Have your data erased or blocked — you can delete your account anytime in Settings → Delete my account;
  • Data portability, getting your data in a commonly used electronic format;
  • Claim damages if you are harmed by inaccurate, unlawfully obtained or unauthorised use of your data; and
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

To use these rights, email s2023101800@firstasia.edu.ph. We may ask you to confirm your identity and will respond within a reasonable time, and in any case within the period required by law.

10. Device storage

Sidekick stores some information on your device so it works offline and keeps you signed in. It does not use cookies or tracking. See the Cookie & Storage Notice.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will update the effective date above and tell you in the app before the change applies.
